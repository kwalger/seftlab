Tadreebak (تدريبك) - Logo Pack

Mark: a sharp arrow pointing to the upper-left (the direction of progress in right-to-left reading).
The head is cut at 45 degrees like a blade, and the shaft is a separate piece with a precise gap.

  tadreebak-logo-horizontal ........... Main website logo: symbol (no container) + name. For light backgrounds.
  tadreebak-logo-horizontal-white ..... Same, for dark backgrounds.
  tadreebak-logo-badge / -badge-white . Version with the symbol inside a rounded blue square.
  tadreebak-social-512.png ............ Social profile picture 512x512 (full-bleed; platforms apply their own circle mask).
  tadreebak-icon (svg / -512.png) ..... App/website icon (rounded square).
  tadreebak-mark-blue / -white / -navy  Symbol only, transparent background.
  favicon.svg, favicon-32/64.png ...... Browser tab icon (bolder for tiny sizes).
  apple-touch-icon-180.png ............ iPhone home-screen icon.
(PNG files come in @1x / @2x / @4x with transparent backgrounds.)

Colors: Brand blue #2F6BFF (icon gradient #4C88FF -> #1E55E4) | Ink #0B1A44 | Muted text #5A6C94
Arabic wordmark: IBM Plex Sans Arabic SemiBold (converted to outlines)
